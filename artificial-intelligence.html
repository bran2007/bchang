Artificial Intelligence

My thesis on AI, where the industry is headed + concerns.

Artificial intelligence is shifting from a tool that generates information to decision-making infrastructure. This change will reshape labor, science, education, and governance, while creating new risks around concentration of power, truth, and human agency. The defining question is not whether AI becomes more capable. It will. The harder question is whether our institutions, incentives, and values can keep pace. The next decade will be shaped as much by human decisions as by machine intelligence.
