GalaxSea Explorers

Making space education accessible for every student.

When I first started GalaxSea Explorers, I wanted to solve a problem I saw in my own community: many students were fascinated by space, but did not have access to the resources, mentors, or opportunities that could help them explore that curiosity.

Through GalaxSea Explorers, I have worked to make space education more accessible for students from underrepresented and Title I communities. Our team has distributed over 500 hands-on space science kits, created educational materials like the Space Ranger's Guide, and reached more than 262,000 students through outreach programs and partnerships.

Building GalaxSea has allowed me to combine my interests in aerospace, engineering, and education. From designing STEM activities to speaking with students about careers in science, every part of this work has shown me the impact that a simple introduction to a new field can have on a young person's future.

One of the most meaningful parts of this experience has been seeing students who never imagined themselves as future scientists begin asking questions about rockets, planets, and space exploration. These moments remind me why I started GalaxSea in the first place: curiosity should not depend on where someone grows up or what resources they have access to.

As I continue expanding GalaxSea Explorers, I hope to keep creating opportunities for students to see themselves as innovators, researchers, and explorers of the universe.
